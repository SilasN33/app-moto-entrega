# BRASA — Creative Bible

**Hero animation for the Brasa food-delivery platform landing page**
Diorama / isometric miniature world · Pixar + Apple + Stripe quality bar
Format: 16:9 · 4K target · seamless 8–10s hero loop

---

## 0. The name as a creative engine

**Brasa** = *live coal / ember* in Portuguese. It carries heat, the glow of a grill, the warmth of a freshly cooked meal, and the energy of a city that never cools down. This single word dictates every creative decision below:

- **Light is the brand.** Brasa cities glow from within — windows, street lamps and delivery routes all read like embers waking up.
- **Warmth over chrome.** Matte-white architecture catching a warm, low sun. Never cold, never clinical.
- **Movement = combustion.** Routes ignite and travel like sparks; the city "catches fire" with orders in the best possible sense.

## 1. Ten distinct concepts

Each concept is a different *spine* for the same diorama. They are designed so the strongest ideas can be combined.

**C1 — "The City Wakes"**
At dusk the model is dark and still. One restaurant lights up, then a chain reaction ripples outward until the whole city glows. Story = demand igniting. Pure ember metaphor.

**C2 — "One Order, One Journey"**
We follow a single glowing order from a phone tap → kitchen → rider → doorstep. Intimate, Apple-ad clarity. Everything else is soft background life.

**C3 — "The Living Network"**
Top-down hero where orange routes weave the city into a single illuminated circuit board. Story = Brasa as the nervous system of the city. Stripe-grade abstraction.

**C4 — "Golden Hour Glide"**
No explicit narrative — a slow, continuous camera glide through impossibly beautiful streets at sunset, riders threading through. Mood-first, Airbnb storytelling.

**C5 — "Hologram Bloom"**
A glassmorphism phone UI rises out of the diorama as a hologram; tapping it sends a spark down into the streets. Tech-poetic, Arc Browser energy.

**C6 — "Constellation of Cravings"**
Each active order is a tiny floating light above a building. As night falls the city becomes a constellation; routes are the lines between stars. Highly premium, minimal.

**C7 — "Miniature Marketplace"**
Showcases the ecosystem breadth — restaurant, pharmacy, market, café — each a charming model vignette, stitched together by routes. Product-credibility forward.

**C8 — "Spark Relay"**
Riders pass a glowing "ember packet" between intersections like a relay; the camera hands off from rider to rider. Kinetic, continuous, loop-friendly.

**C9 — "Breathing Diorama"**
The whole model gently "breathes" — trees sway, lights pulse, traffic loops — built from the ground up as a perfect seamless ambient loop with no hard start/end.

**C10 — "Ember to Logo"**
The entire city's route-lights slowly converge and resolve into the Brasa ember mark. Built specifically to nail Scene 08's logo reveal as the loop's punctuation.

### Winning combination → **C1 + C4 + C9 + C10**
*"The city wakes during a golden-hour glide, breathing as a seamless loop that resolves into the Brasa ember."*
This fuses an emotional spine (wake-up), a premium camera language (glide), loop integrity (breathing), and a branded payoff (ember logo) — covering Scenes 01, 04, 07 and 08 of the brief in one continuous move, with 02/03/05/06 readable as life happening inside the glide.

## 2. Five cinematographic storyboards

Each is a complete shot design; SB-3 is selected.

**SB-1 — The Reveal Push (single take)**
0–2s wide aerial, city dark. 2–5s slow push-in as restaurants ignite block by block. 5–8s routes bloom outward. 8–10s pull-up reveals full glowing grid → ember mark. *Energy: rising.*

**SB-2 — The Intimate Follow**
Locks onto one rider from behind at street level, racing through the diorama; depth-of-field keeps the hero sharp while the city melts into bokeh. Loop hides the cut at an underpass. *Energy: kinetic, personal.*

**SB-3 — The Orbital Glide (SELECTED)**
A continuous, gentle orbit-and-descend around the city center. The camera never stops. It descends just enough to feel the streets breathe, then rises back to the wide as routes converge into the logo. The orbit makes the loop *invisible* — the end frame is the start frame from a hair different angle. *Energy: serene, premium, endless.*

**SB-4 — The Layer Lift**
Camera holds a 3/4 iso angle while the city reveals in layers: ground traffic → mid buildings lighting → upper holographic UI → top route-constellation. Compositing-forward, Linear.app precision. *Energy: structured.*

**SB-5 — The Hand-off Relay**
Camera rides ember packets from rider to rider across four micro-vignettes (café, market, pharmacy, home), each a beat of the ecosystem, looping back to the café. *Energy: playful, ecosystem-proof.*

**Why SB-3:** A non-stop orbital glide is the single most reliable way to (a) feel expensive, (b) showcase the whole diorama, and (c) guarantee a seamless loop — the camera path is a closed ring. It also degrades gracefully in AI image-to-video, where slow continuous moves hold quality far better than cuts.

## 3. Five lighting studies

**L1 — Blue-Hour Ignition:** cool deep-blue base so every ember light pops at maximum contrast. Dramatic, but risks feeling cold against the warm brand.

**L2 — Full Golden Hour (SELECTED):** low warm sun raking across matte-white models, long soft shadows, gentle ambient occlusion, subtle bloom on every light source. The brief's exact request and the truest expression of "Brasa." Warm, friendly, premium.

**L3 — Overcast Studio:** soft even shutter-light, architectural-model neutrality, lights as the only color. Elegant but emotionally flat for a hero.

**L4 — Night Bloom:** full dark, city as pure constellation of ember points, volumetric haze, heavy bloom. Gorgeous for the climax but loses the matte-white material story.

**L5 — Dawn-to-Dusk Drift:** light subtly travels across the loop. Beautiful in theory, but a moving sun fights seamless looping — reserved as a stretch goal only.

**Selected — L2 with a whisper of L4 at the climax:** hold golden hour for the body of the loop, then let the ember routes bloom slightly brighter as they converge into the logo, then ease back. Warmth throughout, a controlled glow at the peak.

## 4. Five art directions

**A1 — Clay Diorama:** soft matte clay/ceramic models, rounded edges, tilt-shift miniature feel. Friendly, tactile, Pixar-adjacent. **(SELECTED base.)**

**A2 — Paper Craft:** folded-paper city, charming but reads craft-fair, not premium-tech.

**A3 — Frosted Glass / Glassmorphism:** semi-translucent buildings, light passing through. Stunning for UI holograms but expensive to keep legible across a whole city.

**A4 — Architectural Maquette:** true scale-model realism — basswood, acrylic, foam — with visible model craftsmanship. Apple-keynote credible.

**A5 — Neon-Minimal Iso:** Linear/Arc flat-but-dimensional, thin emissive lines, near-monochrome with ember accent. Ultra-modern, very brandable.

**Selected — A1 clay base + A3 for UI moments + A5 for the route system:** matte clay city for warmth and tactility, glassmorphism reserved exclusively for the floating phone/UI hologram so it stays special, and neon-minimal emissive lines for the routes so the network reads cleanly from above. This layering keeps the world charming up close and graphic from the hero wide.

## 5. The locked combination

> **Concept:** C1+C4+C9+C10 — *a golden-hour city waking, breathing, resolving into the ember.*
> **Camera:** SB-3 — continuous orbital glide-and-descend, closed-ring loop.
> **Lighting:** L2 golden hour, with a controlled L4 ember bloom at the logo climax.
> **Art direction:** A1 matte-clay diorama, A3 glass only for the UI hologram, A5 emissive route lines.
> **Brand:** Brasa ember mark, ember-orange signature, warm matte-white world.

This is the single most premium, loop-safe, on-brief reading of the project — and the one we build the key frame and final video from.

## 6. Production notes (image → video pipeline)

1. **Logo** generated first as a minimalist ember mark (flat, scalable, on light and dark).
2. **Hero key frame** generated at top quality (4K-capable model) matching the locked combination — this becomes the video's start frame, guaranteeing art-direction control.
3. **Video** produced as image-to-video with a slow orbital glide prompt, 8–10s, 16:9, tuned for a seamless loop.
4. **Verification** pass on motion smoothness, loop seam, palette fidelity, and "expensive" feel before delivery.
